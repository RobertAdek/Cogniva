Cogniva is a private, AI-assisted study workspace that turns notes, documents, images, and recordings into source-grounded flashcards and quizzes. Learners can verify each AI-generated card against the original material, approve it before studying, and use spaced review and progress tracking to strengthen retention. It’s designed for students and self-directed learners who want to study their own materials with AI support while keeping the final say over what they learn.
